01 / Our story

One world.
One health.

Bangladesh's One Health movement took an important step at CVASU with the Chittagong Declaration in 2008. In 2015, CVASU established the One Health Institute as a common platform for collaborative education, research and training across human, animal and environmental health.

04 / Research

Questions that
cross borders.

Our research responds to health priorities where people, animals, food systems and environments meet.

01

Emerging infections

Preparedness begins before an outbreak.

Studying zoonotic and emerging diseases, strengthening surveillance and developing the people and systems needed to respond.

02

AMR + food systems

Safer food. Smarter use of antimicrobials.

Investigating antimicrobial resistance, residues and risks across animal-origin food and the wider food system.

03

Climate + vectors

Following risk through a changing environment.

Building knowledge on vector-borne disease, climate-linked health threats and ecological drivers in Bangladesh.
